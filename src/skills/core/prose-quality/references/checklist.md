# Prose checklist

Answer each with pass or fail. Fix every fail and run the list again.

## Meaning

1. Every claim, number and example traces to the draft or the evidence; nothing
   was invented.
2. The writer would recognise the voice: vocabulary, bluntness, humor and
   uncertainty survive.
3. Cutting is proportional to the slop; strong sentences were left alone.
4. Code, identifiers, quotes and error strings are unchanged.

## Words and sentences

5. No decorative inflated vocabulary, empty adverbs or filler phrases remain
   (technical senses excepted).
6. No binary contrast, negative listing, throat-clearing opener, colon reveal or
   rhetorical setup.
7. No -ing tail, importance puffery, metadiscourse or weasel attribution; each
   unsourced claim is flagged, not invented.
8. Actors are named; no inanimate thing performs a human verb.
9. One term per thing; no synonym cycling.
10. Every generic sentence passed the portability test or was cut.

## Shape

11. No fake-profound kicker, recap ending or sycophantic frame.
12. No decorative formatting; em dashes are rare or absent.
13. Sentence length and paragraph shape vary; no stacked punchy fragments.

## Delivery

14. Edit mode returns the full draft plus `What changed`; detect mode returns
    quoted findings only, with no rewrite, score or authorship verdict.
